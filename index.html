Common questions

How do I know the price?

Prices change with bird size, age, and quantity. Message us on WhatsApp and we will give you the current price.

What is the difference between broilers and layers?

Broilers are raised for meat and grow fast. Layers are raised for eggs and are also sold as hens.

Why choose local chicken?

Local chickens are known for firm meat and a rich traditional taste, popular for pepper soup and festive meals.

Do you sell foreign and local turkey?

Yes. Foreign turkey is larger with more meat, while local turkey has a traditional flavour. Both suit celebrations.
